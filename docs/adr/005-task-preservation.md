# ADR 005-task-preservation: Preserve task operations through adaptation

Status: Accepted.

## Decision

Small-screen alternative must preserve the operations needed to complete the task, not just field reachability. Exact comparison may retain a two-axis table. Explicit choice, hard constraints, focus/drafts and accessibility cannot be traded away for an attractive score.

## Consequences

Acceptance depends on the applicable implementation tasks and evidence in docs/20-execution-plan.md. This ADR is a design decision, not a claim of completed code. A replacement needs explicit alternatives, migration and conformance tests.
