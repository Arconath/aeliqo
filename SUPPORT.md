# Support and maturity

The shipped item at this stage is an implementation kit. Product, browser, provider and deployment evidence are tracked separately. Use docs/26-observations.md and the active checkpoint to determine what was actually verified.

Public issue/discussion availability must be checked in the repository; the observed repository had Issues disabled. Do not claim a monitored support queue. Documentation, bug reports and reproducible synthetic examples should avoid sensitive data. Commercial support is a proposed separate offering until an actual agreement exists.
