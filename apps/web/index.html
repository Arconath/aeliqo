THE TRUST BOUNDARY IS PART OF THE DESIGN

Useful without pretending to be the authority.

Aeliqo validates semantics, queries, results, and presentation plans. Your application still owns its data, authenticated principal, routes, and business execution.

  • Manual authoring stays fully supported.
  • AI proposals are typed, bounded, and untrusted.
  • The framework and manual controls work without AI.
