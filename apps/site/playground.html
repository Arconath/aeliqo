Use your DeepSeek key

Your browser sends the prompt, selected synthetic scenario context, available tool definitions, and any bounded synthetic metadata returned by tools directly to DeepSeek using deepseek-flash. DeepSeek bills usage to your account. Aeliqo does not receive or proxy your key. The fixed endpoint is https://api.deepseek.com/chat/completions; custom endpoints are not supported.

The key is held only in this page's memory and is cleared on disconnect, reset, or page close.
