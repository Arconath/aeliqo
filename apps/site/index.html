THE BOUNDARY, IN ONE SENTENCE

Agents propose. Your app decides.

An agent may submit a typed intent — it can never provide HTML, JavaScript, SQL, credentials, permissions, or an unregistered view.

  • Your app keeps its data, identity, routes, and actions.
  • Manual authoring and controls work with no AI at all.
  • Invalid or stale proposals are rejected before anything renders.
